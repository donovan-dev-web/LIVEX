05 Architecture

A pipeline, three responsibilities.

LIVEX links three components by explicit contracts. The world produces traces, observation measures them, rendering makes them visible — never mixing roles.

5.1 Overview

From world to perception.

world SYNE traces ECHOS measures PRISM perception

Each stage is independent and versioned separately. Any component can be replaced without breaking others, as long as transport and persistence contracts are respected.

5.2 Components

Three blocks, three declared states.

SYNE

Deterministic multi-agent simulation engine: rules, world, ticks and bit-for-bit replayability from a seed.
