08 Architecture · PRISM

Rendering.

PRISM (Perceptual Rendering & Interactive Simulation Module) gives form and presence to the simulated world. It is the least advanced component of the project, and we say so.

◆In development Unreal project and PRISM-LDK plugin

In development: the PRISM rendering experience is out of scope for V0.1. No playable version is claimed.

8.1 Role

Making dynamics visible.

Where SYNE computes and ECHOS measures, PRISM makes things perceptible: it transforms the world state and its events into an observable and interactive scene. It consists of an Unreal project and an integration plugin, PRISM-LDK.

8.2 State

The youngest component.

PRISM is a work-in-progress. No timeline is promised. The project's roadmap is authoritative, and rendering is not listed for V0.1.

Do not assume that local protections in the engine or mock secure distribution of the PRISM project. See SECURITY.md.
