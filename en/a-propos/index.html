Where LIVEX comes from.

LIVEX was born of a question, more than a plan. Here, in the author's words, is what it seeks to explore.

Can we build a virtual environment governed by fundamental laws, within which autonomous entities produce behaviours and structures that were not explicitly programmed?

That is the question that gave rise to LIVEX. Before being a development project, LIVEX was born of a curiosity for science fiction and LitRPG worlds: worlds that seem alive because their entities have needs, perceptions and the ability to decide.

I am a developer and 3D visual artist. My background led me to both sides of the same problem: how to represent a world, and how to build what makes it work. LIVEX arose from the meeting of visual creation, development and curiosity about complex systems.

My vision is simple: do not program the behaviours I want to observe, but build the environment and the laws that can allow them to emerge. And if something interesting appears, it is not enough to show it — it must be possible to observe it, measure it, analyse it and, as far as possible, reproduce it.

— Donovan Chartrain
