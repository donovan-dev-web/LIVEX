21 About · AI transparency

Say what the AI did, and what it did not do.

Two different things carry the word “AI” in LIVEX: the assistance of generative tools during development, and the behaviour of the entities of the simulated world. It is important never to confuse them.

21.1 Principle

A solo project, augmented and responsible.

LIVEX is carried by a single person. It would have been hardly achievable alone without the help of artificial-intelligence tools, which speed up documentation, verification and certain repetitive tasks. This is a fact, and concealing it would be a form of dishonesty.

This page describes where these tools intervene, how their output is controlled, and what they are not in the project.

21.2 Tools and frameworks of use

Guarded assistance, never blind.

PhaseUse of AI
VerificationReview and consistency checks on passages already written.
RewritingHelp with clarity and concision, subject to the author's approval.
DocumentationCorrection and formatting of repository documents.
CodeA few functions, reviewed and tested like any other contribution.
Runs and calibrationHelp preparing and analysing simulation runs.
ReviewDouble-checking of proposals before integration.
21.3 Written by hand, then verified

The decision remains human.

Design choices, architecture trade-offs and sensitive wordings remain written by the author, then verified. Nothing is published on the sole basis of an automatic generation: any claim about a capability, a figure or a property is confronted with the real state of the project before being released.

21.4 Safeguards

What bounds the assistance.

Human review

Any assisted output goes through a human review before being kept.

Tests

The code is submitted to the same test suites as the rest of the project; no preferential treatment.

Traceability

The repository, its commits and its changelog remain the verifiable record of the work.

Register of claims

Sensitive claims on the site carry a dated internal source, regardless of how they were written.

21.5 What the project is not

The agents are not language models.

The entities of LIVEX are not language models, and the world is not “learned” by an AI. Their cognition rests on explicit, deterministic and inspectable rules. No generative model intervenes at the heart of the simulation: the question LIVEX asks — what local, replayable laws produce — requires a cognition that is poor but transparent, not an opaque network.

See also the distinction between simulated agent, learning AI and scripted character inEmergence explained.
