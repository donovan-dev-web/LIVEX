20.1 About · Author

Who is behind LIVEX.

LIVEX started from a question and a journey. Here is, in its author's own words, what he seeks to explore — and where to find the rest of his work.

Can one build a virtual environment governed by fundamental laws, in which autonomous entities produce behaviours and structures that were never explicitly programmed?

I am Donovan Chartrain, a fullstack web & mobile developer and 3D artist. My background combines more than 3 years of web development in industry and more than 10 years of design, 3D and visual production. This dual skill set led me to both sides of the same problem: how to represent a world, and how to build what makes it work.

LIVEX grew from that meeting of visual creation, development and curiosity about complex systems. Before it was a development project, it grew from a curiosity for science-fiction and LitRPG worlds: worlds that feel alive because their entities have needs, perceptions and the ability to decide.

My vision is simple: not to program the behaviours I wish to observe, but to build the environment and the laws that may let them emerge. And if something interesting appears, it is not enough to show it — one must be able to observe, measure, analyse and, as far as possible, reproduce it.

— Donovan Chartrain
