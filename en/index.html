01 The premise

Make legible what no one wrote.

LIVEX seeks to make observable a collective behaviour that was never programmed by hand. Its method rests on two requirements: a deterministic engine (the same starting point replays the same run, bit for bit) and a clean separation between what happens (the engine) and what is said about it (the observation).

  • Deterministic — same seed, same configuration, same trajectory.
  • Separated — simulate, measure, make visible: three independent layers.
  • Documented — every published figure has a dated internal source.
